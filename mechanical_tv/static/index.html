Apparatus Configuration

Main Computer: Raspberry Pi 4 Model B

Motor Controller: Adafruit DC & Stepper Motor HAT (PCA9685 I2C @ 0x60, TB6612 dual H-bridge)

Drive Motor: STEPPERONLINE NEMA 17 Bipolar Stepper (200 full steps / 400 half-steps per rev)

Index Sensor: Photoelectric opto-interrupter on BCM GPIO 4

Light Source: 3W High-power LED modulated via L298N on BCM GPIO 21 / SPI MOSI

Thermal Protection: Hardware stall watchdog forces LED power cut if disc rotation falls below 1.0 FPS or opto pulses cease.
